## Engineering Projects

### MirageMind AI: Planetary Life Signature Estimation System
`Simulation-validated prototype` · 2025 – present · [Repository](https://github.com/meetdesai2k7/MirageMind-AI)

An experimental environmental-intelligence and habitability-assessment framework. An ESP32 gathers readings from DHT22, MQ135 and HC-SR04 sensors and fuses them over UART at the edge. A rule-based decision engine produces a **Life Possibility Score** and classifies the environment as *Hostile*, *Potentially Habitable* or *Habitable*. The approach was validated in ROS 2 Humble and Gazebo simulation, with a real-time Pygame radar-style dashboard.

It is not a life-detection instrument and does not detect organisms; it estimates how habitable measured conditions appear under a defined rule set.

<img src="assets/diagram-miragemind.svg" alt="System overview of MirageMind AI: three sensors feed an ESP32 over UART, then a rule-based decision engine produces a Life Possibility Score classified as Hostile, Potentially Habitable or Habitable, shown on a Pygame radar dashboard and validated in ROS 2 Humble and Gazebo." width="100%">

**Stack:** ESP32 · Python · ROS 2 Humble · Gazebo · Pygame · OpenCV · Linux

### ASTRAVIA: Autonomous Scientific Terrain Reconnaissance & Astrobiology Vehicle
`Concept` · 2026 – present

A planetary-exploration vehicle concept combining multimodal sensing, edge AI, sensor fusion and computer vision. I am designing a scientific-assessment framework that generates a Life Possibility Score and proposing target-prioritisation logic based on evidence, risk and energy constraints.

This is a design effort. There is no completed vehicle, and nothing here has been tested outside software or desk study.
